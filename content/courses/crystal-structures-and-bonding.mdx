This course is a first look at the physics and chemistry behind every material you have ever touched. We start with the bonds that hold atoms together, then build up the repeating structures those bonds create, and finish with the defects — missing atoms, dislocations, and grain boundaries — that explain why real materials behave the way they do.

<Callout type="note" title="Prerequisites">
  High-school chemistry and basic algebra are enough. We introduce the few
  equations (density, defect concentrations, Schmid's law) from scratch in each
  lesson, so nothing here requires prior materials science.
</Callout>
