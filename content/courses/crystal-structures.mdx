This course is a hands-on introduction to crystallography for materials science students. You will learn how to describe the repeating arrangement of atoms in a crystal using unit cells, the 14 Bravais lattices, and Miller indices.

<Callout type="tip" title="What you'll need">
  No prior crystallography knowledge is required, but a tolerance for
  three-dimensional geometry helps!
</Callout>
